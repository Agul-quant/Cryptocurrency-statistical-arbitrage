# Research story and evaluation chronology

## The original question

The supplied Wall Street Quants assignment asked whether cryptocurrency
momentum or reversal patterns could predict returns after execution costs.
It did not prescribe eight coins, a larger initial coin pool, or an asset screen.

The original study configured BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, LTCUSDT, ADAUSDT, LINKUSDT,
DOGEUSDT, DOTUSDT and SOLUSDT directly. They are a purposive fixed survivor
sample. No retained record supports an initial candidate count, exclusion list,
top-market-cap ranking, liquidity cutoff or point-in-time eligibility rule.
Common Binance USDT pricing and long archived coverage are practical advantages,
not documented historical selection criteria. The 18 original candidates were
strategy parameter configurations, not candidate coins.

December 2021 provided warm-up. Development used 2022-2023, parameter selection
used 2024 net Sharpe at 20 bps, and 2025 was initially evaluated after freezing
those selections. Both selected original controls lost money net of costs.

## Wider horizons came afterward

The subsequent 156-configuration hourly time-scale scan expanded lookbacks
and rebalancing cadences after the original 2025 outcomes had been inspected.
Its configurations were ranked on 2024, but 2025 could no longer serve as an
untouched test of the expanded research design. It remains retrospective
exploratory evidence. The 28-day-lookback weekly reversal reference belongs
to this preserved scan, not to the original 18-configuration winning pair.

## The expanded study

Five extensions investigated signal rank correlations, lower-turnover
cross-sectional momentum, long/cash and theoretical long/short time-series
trend, fixed BTC-factor residual-spread reversal, and diagnostic-only regimes.
The universe stayed fixed. The historical years had already been examined.

Code, candidates, thresholds, monthly-update rules and selections were frozen
on October 6, 2026 at 12:33:04 EDT. First recorded access to 2026 prices followed
at 12:33:34 EDT. January 1-September 30, 2026 was new to this analysis when
first evaluated: a 273-day historical holdout, not prospective live trading.
Reported evaluation boundaries and weekly trading schedules use UTC.

The weekly reversal reference's nine-month net return at 20 bps was 17.11%,
Sharpe 1.53 and observed hourly maximum drawdown 8.87%. This favorable outcome
does not remove broader search, survivor-universe or winner-focus bias.

## The five-minute pilot

Actual five-minute candles tested momentum/reversal with 5/15/30-minute
lookbacks and 5/15/60-minute rebalancing. All 18 configurations and seven
cost/delay cases were retained across four periods (504 measurements).
All periods are exploratory or retrospective, including January-September
2026, because those calendar outcomes had already been seen at coarser
resolution. Smaller bars did not remove turnover costs.

## Portfolio meaning

Rank-based cross-sectional reversal buys recent relative losers and shorts
relative winners. The weekly reference uses trailing 672-hour returns and
Monday 00:00 UTC decisions, with demeaned rank/L1 weights and 50% long/50%
short target exposure when ranks are distinct. Long/cash and time-series
long/short trend are different models; the fixed residual model is a separate
BTC-hedged spread experiment. Dollar neutrality does not ensure beta neutrality.

No new prices, coin screening, selection or trading research was performed
while packaging this local repository. Historical freeze timestamps were not
reset. The raw course assignment remains private and is not redistributed.
