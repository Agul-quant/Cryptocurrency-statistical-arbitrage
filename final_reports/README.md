# Final reports

## Main finding: performance did not persist across periods

All four development-selected A strategies lost in 2024–2025, and all five
validation-selected B strategies lost in January–September 2026. The separate
fixed all-22 studies also did not establish persistent cash-relative profitability:

| Separate fixed study | Development net | Validation net | Jan–Sep 2026 net |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 28-day weekly momentum | +5.61% | −17.43% | −20.94% |
| 28-day weekly reversal | −33.59% | −15.66% | +9.91% |

These fixed studies are not ranked alternatives, a combined strategy or a new
selection. Each retains its own three-period tests and all fee/delay sensitivities.
All three reports contain a separately labelled section, period details and
full-precision companion exports. Exactly three final PDFs remain.

Read the three reports in this order:

1. **Development and robustness** — `01_development/01_development_and_robustness.pdf`: 2022–2023, the full strategy/basket grid and development robustness.
2. **Validation and frozen selection** — `02_validation/02_validation_and_frozen_selection.pdf`: 2024–2025, unchanged Track A tests and explicitly validation-informed Track B selections.
3. **2026 OOS evaluation and conclusions** — `03_2026_evaluation/03_2026_evaluation_and_conclusions.pdf`: 1 January–30 September 2026, the nine frozen strategies, corrected inference and explanatory diagnostics.

**Data:** Binance public Data Vision spot-USDT one-hour klines. The complete normalized study dataset covers all 22 screening-universe economic slots and 43,824 UTC close endpoints, from 1 October 2021 at 01:00 through 1 October 2026 at 00:00. [Data sources, field definitions, inventory and coverage](../docs/DATA.md).

Each report has an offline HTML counterpart. Tables/exports retain full-precision strategy results, per-coin gross/fee/net contributions, joint eight-coin alpha/betas, confidence intervals and BTC-only comparisons.

2026 observations had been examined before the later factor and cohort diagnostics. Those diagnostics are explanatory, not independent new confirmation or live performance. Track B validation results remain selection-conditioned. No new winner or deployment approval is claimed.

The fixed studies use already-examined dates and do not provide fresh independent
confirmation. Momentum retains its disclosed pre-input-access chronology exception;
reversal met its new-study pre-decode seal. The original nine financial results,
acceptance flags and existing factor/cash evidence remain unchanged. See the
[methods](../docs/METHODS.md#separate-fixed-all-22-momentum-and-reversal-studies)
and the two protocol documents for interpretation and provenance.
