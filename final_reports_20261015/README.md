# Final reading list: three reports

These three reports replace the earlier multi-report reading list. They consolidate the ten post-restart analytical studies, the selected/reference presentation edition, and the approved OOS protocol into one development, one validation, and one 2026 evaluation narrative.

1. **Development and robustness** — universe and liquidity tiers, complete strategy grid, accounting/cost conventions, 2022–2023 findings and robustness before selection.
   Final PDF: `01_development/01_development_and_robustness.pdf`
2. **Validation and frozen selection** — original development-only selections, 2024–2025 outcomes, selected/reference diagnosis, full-grid transfer, Track B and the unchanged nine-strategy shortlist.
   Final PDF: `02_validation/02_validation_and_frozen_selection.pdf`
3. **2026 evaluation and conclusions** — the approved January–September partial-year evaluation, frozen-nine results and inference, cohort screening, controls/survivors diagnosis, execution limitations and conclusions.
   Final PDF: `03_2026_evaluation/03_2026_evaluation_and_conclusions.pdf`

Each report also has the same-content interactive/offline HTML edition in its own directory. Those formats are not additional reports. Technical source tables and detailed archival diagnostics remain supporting evidence, not extra reports in the final reading list.

## Preservation

Original studies remain unchanged under `research/`; no reports or results are deleted or silently replaced. This directory is a presentation-only consolidation: no market prices are loaded or acquired, no strategy is rerun, and no basket, parameter, selection, statistical decision, or 2026 winner is changed. The existing retrospective/search/shorting/execution limitations remain.

`publication_protocol.json`, `publication_freeze.json`, `audit/` and the final delivery manifest document source checks, review and preservation. They are provenance/navigation records, not a fourth research report.
