Crypto statistical arbitrage: momentum and reversal¶

An executed research companion to the supplied Wall Street Quants class project. The notebook uses cached, checksum-audited Binance hourly spot inputs; it never downloads or reselects strategies. Shorts and close execution are theoretical.

tl;dr¶

None of the frozen selections was profitable after the prescribed 20 bps cost in the 2025 test.

  • Mom 168h/24h: -40.38% net test return, Sharpe -2.75, maximum drawdown 42.60%.
  • Rev 1h/24h: -57.72% net test return, Sharpe -4.43, maximum drawdown 59.02%.

Context & Methods¶

Question: do price continuation or reversal patterns survive the course's 20 bps one-way trading cost? We compare an 18-configuration predeclared grid. Development is 2022-2023, selection uses 2024 net Sharpe, and 2025 is the frozen test. December 2021 supplies warm-up.

Documented data exception, fixed before experiments: Binance spot halted on March 24, 2023 at 11:27 UTC and resumed at 14:00. We bridge the observed 11:00 and 15:00 closes as one four-hour holding interval: existing exposure remains invested; no fills, costs, or valuations at 12:00-14:00; no price imputation or dropped reopening return. Exact elapsed-hour signal endpoints are required; missing denominators defer trading. Daily returns preserve 24 elapsed hours; drawdown is observed-endpoint during this interval. Sixteen close-time metadata anomalies are audited, including one daily-archive metadata repair with identical OHLCV and fifteen allowlisted shortened timestamps. 2025 is complete.

Key Assumptions¶

  • Eight fixed survivors: BTC, ETH, LTC, ADA, LINK, DOGE, DOT, SOL in USDT.
  • Trailing price-ratio returns; momentum follows winners, reversal follows losers.
  • Demeaned ranks / absolute-rank sum: dollar-neutral individual families have unit gross, zero net exposure, with average ranks for ties. Blending positions may reduce gross exposure.
  • Rebalances every 1, 4, or 24 UTC hours; targets held between decisions.
  • A completed signal earns the next observed interval return (normally one hour, lag 1); lag 2 is an extra-delay stress.
  • Full L1 weight-change turnover, including entry and liquidation; no drift, funding, borrow, margin, capacity, or guaranteed limit fills.
  • Daily compounding uses UTC interval-days (a midnight endpoint belongs to the prior day), 365-day annualization and zero risk-free rate; BTC alpha/beta uses daily OLS + HAC(7).

4. Demonstrate the portfolio calculation by hand¶

At one scheduled test timestamp, recompute trailing returns, ranks and weights directly. Positive weights sum to 0.5, negative weights to -0.5; this is not a dollar-neutral guarantee under weight drift. The target uses no future price.

example = selected[0]
L = example['lookback_h']
R = example['rebalance_h']
t = next(t for t in prices.index if t >= pd.Timestamp('2025-01-02', tz='UTC') and t.hour % R == 0)
raw_signal = prices.loc[t] / prices.shift(L).loc[t] - 1
signal = raw_signal if example['family'] == 'momentum' else -raw_signal
rank = signal.rank(method='average')
centered = rank - rank.mean()
weights = centered / centered.abs().sum() if centered.abs().sum() else centered * 0
example_targets = generate_targets(prices, example['family'], L, R)
np.testing.assert_allclose(weights, example_targets.loc[t], atol=1e-14)
assert abs(weights.sum()) < 1e-14
assert np.isclose(weights.abs().sum(), 1)
display(pd.DataFrame({'trailing_return': raw_signal, 'signal': signal, 'rank': rank, 'weight': weights}).round(5))
print('Scheduled UTC decision:', t)
print('Long / short / gross:', weights.clip(lower=0).sum(), weights.clip(upper=0).sum(), weights.abs().sum())

6. Reproduce the untouched test, then reconcile the saved evidence¶

Generate the same frozen targets from cached inputs and simulate 2025 independently of the saved runner-return table. Also reconstruct gross returns and entry/change/exit costs directly from held positions. Finally reproduce daily metrics and verify every saved test return, equity, drawdown, and turnover. No candidate is reselected.

targets_by_id = {
    s['strategy_id']: generate_targets(prices, s['family'], s['lookback_h'], s['rebalance_h'])
    for s in selected
}
if selection['combination_eligible']:
    targets_by_id['combination_50_50'] = sum(
        selection['combination_weights'][sid] * targets_by_id[sid]
        for sid in selection['combination_weights'])
benchmark = prices['BTCUSDT'].pct_change(fill_method=None)
reproduced = {}
checks = []
for sid, targets in targets_by_id.items():
    result = simulate(prices, targets, '2025-01-01', '2026-01-01', 20, 1)
    frame = result.frame
    manual_gross = (result.held_weights * prices.pct_change(fill_method=None).reindex(frame.index)).sum(axis=1)
    changes = result.held_weights.diff()
    changes.iloc[0] = result.held_weights.iloc[0]
    manual_turnover = changes.abs().sum(axis=1)
    manual_turnover.iloc[-1] += result.held_weights.iloc[-1].abs().sum()
    manual_net = manual_gross - manual_turnover * .002
    np.testing.assert_allclose(manual_net, frame['net_return'], atol=1e-14)
    saved = returns.loc[returns['strategy_id'].eq(sid) & returns['split'].eq('test') &
        returns['cost_bps'].eq(20) & returns['execution_lag'].eq(1)].set_index('bar_end').sort_index()
    assert frame.index.equals(saved.index)
    for column in ['gross_return', 'net_return', 'turnover', 'cost', 'equity', 'drawdown']:
        np.testing.assert_allclose(frame[column], saved[column], rtol=1e-11, atol=1e-13)
    stats = summarize(result, benchmark)
    for column in ['total_return', 'cagr', 'annual_return', 'annual_volatility', 'sharpe',
                   'max_drawdown', 'alpha_annual', 'beta']:
        np.testing.assert_allclose(stats[column], test_metrics.loc[sid, column], rtol=1e-10, atol=1e-12)
    # A second independent daily compounding check uses interval-day membership.
    day = (frame.index - pd.Timedelta(nanoseconds=1)).floor('D')

    assert len(frame) == 365 * 24

Takeaways¶

None of the frozen selections was profitable after the prescribed 20 bps cost in the 2025 test.

  • The comparisons separate gross predictability from net economic value. No observed outcome is treated as a guaranteed future profit.
  • A fixed survivor universe, USDT numeraire, exchange-specific prices and an unconstrained short side limit external validity.
  • Close fills, constant costs and target-change turnover are idealizations. Real deployment requires financing, borrow/funding, capacity and live execution study.
  • One untouched year does not eliminate selection bias or regime risk. HAC(7) alpha intervals are not multiple-testing-adjusted.
  • Volume activity is descriptive; information/liquidity explanations remain hypotheses.
